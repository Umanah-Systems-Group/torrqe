Why TORRQE

Machines rarely fail at the motor. They fail after it, in the gears, bearings, lubrication and brakes that turn spin into controlled motion. That is the layer we build.

Teardown

01

A humanoid is mostly joints.

Hips, knees, ankles, shoulders, elbows, wrists, waist and neck. Each one is an actuator that has to be strong, smooth and stiff.

02

Take the shell off.

Under the panels: a frame, a battery and the joints that do the work.

03

Every orange ring is a TORRQE Joint.

Motor, Drive, Core and bearings in one sealed unit. Same family in different sizes, from wrist to hip.

04

Inside the knee.

The motor spins the sun gear. The planets carry the load and act as the bearing, so the joint stays short, stiff and free of backlash.

Braking

Carbon-ceramic lightness. Steel cost.

Brake pads rub only the wave. Thin plates around it pull the heat out. The result is a disc a third the weight of cast iron, made from steel.
