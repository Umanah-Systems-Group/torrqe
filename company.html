Company

Onshoring the motion layer.

TORRQE designs the actuators and the parts inside them in America, and builds them with American precision partners.

Most precision reducers, bearings and actuators come from overseas. The machines that matter most should not depend on that. We are building the American source.

How we build

Designed in America. Built with American precision partners.

We design every part in-house and build early units with qualified US manufacturers. As volume grows, we bring the critical steps in-house.

01 · DESIGN

In-house

Architecture, tolerances, materials and the full motion stack, modelled and simulated before metal is cut.

02 · BUILD

With partners

Precision gear grinding, 5-axis machining and inspection at US shops while we prove the designs.

03 · SCALE

Onshore

Bring the critical steps in-house and build a domestic supply chain for precision motion.
